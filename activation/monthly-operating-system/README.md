# Monthly Activation Operating System

## Governance

This operating system implements the signed monthly retainer under the Services Agreement dated 9 July 2026.

Retainer period: September 2026 to December 2027.

Monthly fee: INR 65,000/month for 16 months.

## Monthly Scope

| Workstream | Signed monthly commitment |
| --- | --- |
| Technical content | 4 technical thought-leadership/document assets |
| Company Page distribution | 8 Company Page posts/assets |
| Leadership/sales-profile content | 8 LinkedIn posts/assets |
| LinkedIn outreach | Outreach to 300 prospects |
| LinkedIn sequence | 1 connection request + 2 follow-ups |
| Cold email | Up to 20,000 campaign email sends |
| Operations | Basic reply triage, CSV/manual lead handoff, monthly optimization, reporting, and guidance |

Additional sends beyond 20,000/month require prior written approval and are charged at INR 0.50 per additional email.

## Monthly Cadence

| Week | Operating focus |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Monthly planning, inputs, approval dependencies, topic selection, prospect/account list readiness |
| Week 2 | Technical asset drafting, Company Page/sales-profile content preparation, sequence setup |
| Week 3 | Distribution, LinkedIn outreach, cold email sending, reply triage, proof-pack handoff |
| Week 4 | Optimization, reporting, learning review, next-month recommendations |

## Pacing And Feasibility Rules

- Outputs depend on Puretronics inputs, approvals, account access, platform limits, data availability, and campaign feasibility.
- Outreach may be paced or adjusted to protect account health and campaign quality.
- Missed output caused by client-side delay does not automatically carry forward unless agreed in writing.
- Additional languages may be incorporated on a reasonable best-efforts basis where commercially appropriate, without committed volume or extra fee obligation.
- SEO, paid advertising, domain/mailbox procurement, list building, enrichment, verification, paid databases, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator are excluded unless separately approved.

## Monthly Closeout

Each month closes with:

- Output count against signed scope
- LinkedIn and cold email activity summary
- Reply/dialogue initiation observations
- Product-family and buyer-segment learning
- Routing or handoff issues
- Scope risks and approvals needed
- Next-month content and outreach recommendations

## Acceptance Criteria

- The operating system matches the signed retainer scope.
- No optional proposal menu is presented as current scope.
- No excluded cost or expansion path is implied as included.
- Reporting language remains non-binding and operational.
