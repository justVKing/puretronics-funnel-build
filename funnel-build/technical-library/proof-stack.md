# Technical Library Proof Stack

## Governance

The technical library supports the signed Wire & Cable funnel scope under the Services Agreement dated 9 July 2026. It is not an expansion of contracted deliverables.

Puretronics must validate product specifications, performance claims, compliance references, diagrams, standards notes, installation assumptions, and technical materials before publication or client-facing use.

## Asset Families

| Asset family | Purpose | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Datasheets | Product-level technical reference and downloadable proof | Landing page, Product Family experiences, sales follow-up |

| Application explainers | Translate product capability into production problems | Landing page, LinkedIn content, cold email nurture |

| Service proof | Support installed-base and support-routing confidence | Service/support path |
| FAQs | Handle recurring technical, commercial-scope, and service questions | Pages, forms, sales enablement |

## Support-Only Proof Rules

Laser accessories, spark tester fault-intelligence/accessory items, Spark Tester Sensitivity Calibrator, and loadcell accessories/integration items may be referenced inside relevant proof assets. They must not be positioned as standalone page topics, independent campaigns, separate forms, separate automations, SEO assets, selectors, calculators, configurators, or additional deliverables without written approval.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every proof asset maps to a canonical Product Family, buyer role, and production stage. Solution Theme is added where useful and never substitutes for Product Family.
- Technical claims are marked for Puretronics validation where needed.
- LTC-PRO appears in all tension-control architecture and integration contexts.
- No proof asset changes signed commercials or scope.
