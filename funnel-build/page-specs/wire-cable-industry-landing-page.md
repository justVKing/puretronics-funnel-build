# Wire & Cable Industry Landing Page Specification

## Governance

Source of truth: signed Services Agreement dated 9 July 2026. The landing page is part of the Puretronics Wire & Cable pilot running 15 July 2026 to 31 December 2027. Build period is July-August 2026; activation period is September 2026 to December 2027.

This page must not imply SEO, paid advertising, extra product pages, additional campaigns, selectors, calculators, configurators, export/OEM/partner/multilingual expansion, or standalone accessory deliverables unless separately approved in writing.

## Page Objective

Create one industry landing page that lets wire and cable manufacturers find Puretronics products by production problem, production stage, and product family, then submit an enquiry or service/support request through the adaptive requirement form.

## Primary Audiences

- Plant heads and production leaders
- Quality and testing leaders
- Maintenance and electrical/instrumentation teams
- Owners, directors, and commercial decision makers
- Purchase and vendor-development teams

## Navigation Model

The first screen should quickly route visitors into one of three discovery paths:

- Find by production problem: diameter variation, insulation faults, HV validation, fire-resistance testing, powder adhesion, tension instability, braking control, butt welding, conductor preheating.
- Find by production stage: extrusion, insulation, sheathing, spark testing, HV testing, FR testing, line control, take-up/pay-off, conductor preheating, welding/splicing.
- Find by solution block: Online Measurement, Spark Testing, HV & Cable Validation, Fire Resistance, Line Stability/Adhesion/Tension Control.

## Required Sections

1. Industry entry section: Puretronics Wire & Cable product universe, with signed-scope caveat.
2. Problem router: short cards for the main line-quality and testing problems.
3. Production-stage router: extrusion, testing, process enhancement, line control, maintenance/service.
4. Five solution blocks: summary cards linking to the solution-block sections/specs.
5. Product coverage strip: signed products and variants only.
6. Technical proof library: datasheets, comparison tables, explainers, diagrams, standards notes, output/integration notes, reports, demo workflows, service proof, FAQs.
7. Adaptive requirement form CTA: sales enquiry and service/support separation.
8. Governance footer: Services Agreement controls scope, commercials, exclusions, approvals, and change control.

## CTA Model

- Primary CTA: Submit technical requirement
- Secondary CTA: Request datasheet or proof pack
- Service CTA: Raise service/support requirement
- Internal routing: product family, problem type, production stage, urgency, buyer role, existing/new customer status

## Signed Product Coverage

Covered primary products and variants include LASER 2008/2008B, LASER-2060/2012/2030, LASER-2010H/2020H/2030H, WTI-90-40, WTI-100-40, Live Spark, Acute Spark, DC Spark, AC HV Tester, DC HV Tester, Fire Resistance Cable Testing System, Inline Induction Wire Preheater variants at 1000/1500/2000 m/min, LSP-G1, LSP-G2, Butt Welding Machine, LTC-PRO Web Tension Controller, AR-85, AR-118, AR-125, AR-ST, LC-AR-60, LC-AR-HD, AX-250, AX-400, and AX-500.

Support items include laser accessories, spark tester fault-intelligence/accessory items, Spark Tester Sensitivity Calibrator, and loadcell accessories/integration items. These are support items only.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Page spec maps CTAs to the adaptive requirement form.
- All product references stay inside signed Wire & Cable scope.
- LTC-PRO appears in line stability, braking, loadcell, WTI, and tension-control contexts.
- No accessory is treated as a standalone page, campaign, form, automation, SEO asset, selector, calculator, configurator, or extra deliverable.
- No commercial terms on the page conflict with Annexure B or the signed Services Agreement.
