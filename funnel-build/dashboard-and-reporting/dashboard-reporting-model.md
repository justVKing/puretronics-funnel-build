# Dashboard And Reporting Model

## Governance

Reporting supports monthly optimization under the signed retainer. It must not imply guaranteed leads, enquiries, sales, refunds, penalties, or payment outcomes.

The dialogue-initiation benchmark is approximately 15% to 20% of total prospects outreached over a reasonable campaign period. This benchmark is non-binding.

## Dashboard Views

| View | Purpose | Core fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product-family demand | Show demand by the canonical Product Family | Product Family, Solution Theme, product/model, count, enquiry quality |
| Buyer segments | Show which roles engage | Buyer role, company type, geography, source, lead classification |
| Problem/process-stage demand | Track actual production problems | Buyer Problem, Manufacturing Flow Stage, urgency, Product Family |
| Enquiry quality | Separate high-intent, technical qualification, proof request, service/support, and out-of-scope | Classification, completeness, owner, next action |
| Routing ownership | Track handoff and response accountability | Owner, SLA target, status, ageing, blocked reason |
| LinkedIn outreach | Monitor signed LinkedIn scope | Prospects outreached, connection requests, follow-ups, replies, dialogue starts |
| Cold email | Monitor signed cold email scope | Sends, deliverability notes, replies, triage status, dialogue starts |
| Monthly activation outputs | Confirm monthly retainer delivery | 4 technical assets, 8 Company Page posts/assets, 8 leadership/sales posts/assets, reports |

## Lead Quality Definitions

| Classification | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| High-intent sales | Product need and next commercial/technical action are clear |
| Technical qualification | Valid requirement with missing specs or configuration details |
| Proof request | Request for datasheet, comparison, explainer, standard note, output note, or demo material |
| Service/support | Installed product support, calibration, maintenance, or troubleshooting |
| Out-of-scope | Excluded product/category/deliverable or expansion request |

## Monthly Reporting Pack

Each month should include:

- Summary of technical assets produced
- Company Page and leadership/sales-profile content output count
- LinkedIn outreach volume against 300-prospect scope
- Cold email send volume against 20,000-send scope
- Dialogue initiation and reply observations
- Product-family and problem-stage demand learning
- Routing and handoff issues
- Recommendations for next month
- Scope risks or approval dependencies
- Next-month content and outreach recommendations

## Acceptance Criteria

- Dashboard fields connect to the adaptive form and funnel page specs.
- Reporting distinguishes sales, proof, service/support, and out-of-scope enquiries.
- Performance benchmark language remains non-binding.
- Monthly outputs match the signed Services Agreement and Annexure B.
